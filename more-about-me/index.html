Outside the lab

More About Me

Outside of research, I find balance through two main passions: music and fitness. My fascination with audio and speech signal processing was initially fueled by a deep love for various music genres—especially Hip-Hop—while training at the gym provides a different kind of creative focus and clarity.

Music

Music has always been a defining part of my life.

My curiosity about how human voices and musical instruments produce and shape sound naturally evolved into an academic interest in speech and audio technologies. Outside of research, I enjoy exploring a wide range of genres, from soulful R&B to lyrical hip-hop.

Recently, I have become particularly drawn to the energy of live performances, often attending concerts by both international and Korean artists in Seoul and beyond. Among them, Mac Miller remains my favorite artist. His music and lyrics have consistently offered me new perspectives, moments of reflection, and a sense of connection that I rarely find elsewhere.

Workout

I’m a dedicated gymrat who loves to train — particularly a mix of weight training and powerlifting. Working out clears my mind and recharges my energy for everything else in life.

I’ve been training consistently since 2020, mainly focusing on upper-body strength, while keeping my leg sessions balanced. My current total SBD (Squat, Bench, Deadlift) is 505 kg.
